# Landing page (frontend/index.html)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: Space Apps judge or reviewer, first and only look, wants to know in seconds what this is, whether it is real, and where to click. Primary action: open the viewer. Proof: the real SPHEREx frame blink, the 28 s walkthrough, the four-track team, the validation report. Constraints: no NASA insignia, required not-operated-by notice, no invented claims or photos, emails shown only as mail buttons. Build path: code-led (no image generation). Direction pinned by the user: NASA web conventions in the existing Space Apps palette and Fira/Overpass type; the roll is skipped.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A mission page, not a startup page. The real SPHEREx blink leads the first screen and the page follows NASA's mission-page grammar (utility bar, plain facts table, prose, story list, mega footer). Refuses the dark-gradient SaaS hero with floating cards and a glowing orb.
OWN-WORLD: Space Apps deep blue ground (#07173F, panels #0A1E4E, hairlines #22407A), neon yellow #EAFE07 only for the primary action, focus and active marks, a darker rocket red #8F2A0D only on the "simulated" flag (white text on #E43700 falls below 4.5:1 at 14px). Fira Sans display, Overpass body, Fira Code only for data values. Hairline rules and tables, no shadows, no cards for structure.
STORY: Visitor sees real infrared data moving, reads that wavelength is the confound and unsafe comparisons are refused, trusts the honest limits, sees who built which track, and opens the viewer.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Utility bar with the not-operated-by notice, header with patch and name and an Open viewer button. Left, headline "See what really moves in the infrared sky" at about 72px, two sentences, yellow Open the viewer and outlined Watch the walkthrough. Right, the real WISE 0855 four-frame blink in a hairline frame with an image caption (data, credit, label). Under both, a four-cell facts table (data, fields, modes, claims).
FORM: Mission page with story list (NASA mission pages), position 1 of my list, pinned by the user. seed: none (direction pinned by the user; the roll was not run)
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
